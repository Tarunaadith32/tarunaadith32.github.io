Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Salary Tracker ("the app") is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains what data the app handles, including data from your Google Account, and how you stay in control of it.

Data you enter

Your salary amounts, budget categories and percentages, and expense entries (description, amount, category and date) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent to the developer. The app has no server of its own, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Google user data

The app accesses Google user data only if you choose Continue with Google to turn on Google Drive backup.

  • Basic profile (name, email address, profile picture). Used only to show which Google Account is connected and to keep backups from different accounts apart. Stored on your device until you disconnect.
  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). Used only to create a Salary Tracker Backups folder in your Drive, save and update backup files there, list them so you can pick one to restore, download the backup you choose, and delete older backups (the app keeps the latest 30 per device). The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Access tokens come from Google's sign-in service and are used only to call the Google Drive API directly from your device over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

How Google user data is used and shared

Google user data is used only to provide backup and restore to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with or transferred to anyone else, not read by any person, and not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Salary Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: kept until you choose Data → Erase all data in the app or uninstall it.
  • In your Google Drive: backups belong to you. The app keeps the latest 30 per device and deletes older ones automatically. You can delete the Salary Tracker Backups folder in Drive at any time.
  • Disconnecting: Google Drive backup → Disconnect in the app signs you out and removes your account details from the device. You can remove the app's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Security

Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Backups travel only between your device and Google over HTTPS.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
