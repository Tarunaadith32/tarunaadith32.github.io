Salary Tracker
Privacy Terms

Plan your salary. Track every spend.

Salary Tracker is a personal Android app that splits your monthly salary into budget categories, logs your expenses and shows what is left in each category as you spend.

What it does

  • Budget categories. Divide your net salary across categories (35-25-15-15-10 by default) and adjust the percentages at any time.
  • Quick spend entry. Add an expense in seconds; matching past entries are suggested as you type.
  • Live balances. Spent, remaining and utilisation for every category, with an alert when a budget is overspent.
  • Month by month. Start each month from the previous month's plan and close finished months to keep them read-only.
  • Search and export. Search, sort and group transactions; export to JSON or CSV.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). Back up automatically to your own Google Drive and restore on a new phone.

Your data stays yours

Everything you enter is stored on your phone. There is no Salary Tracker server, no account to create, no ads and no analytics.

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks Google for access only to the files it creates (drive.file). Backups are saved in a Salary Tracker Backups folder in your Drive, and the app cannot see any of your other files. See the privacy policy for details.

Availability

Salary Tracker is a personal project for Android 8.0 and later, installed directly from an APK. It is not listed on Google Play.
